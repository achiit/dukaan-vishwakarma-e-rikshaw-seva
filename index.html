Started with one rickshaw and my brother’s loan

In 2019 my brother Mohan and I bought one second-hand e-rickshaw. I still remember my first fare, an old lady going to the village temple. She gave me ₹10 extra and I refused it.

Today we run fourteen vehicles. Every driver is from Ratan Chak and every family here knows us by name. If a driver is rude, call me directly.

  • Fixed rates, no meter games or last-minute hike
  • Batteries charged overnight, no breakdowns mid-ride
  • Seat covers washed every Sunday
  • Drivers verified with Aadhaar and local reference
  • Women and senior citizens always get the front-side seat
